# 1. Vision

Digestex is developed as the Digital Infrastructure
for the Global Textile Industry.

The roadmap focuses on delivering value incrementally,
where each phase becomes a complete business product
while supporting the long-term ecosystem vision.

• Deliver usable products

• Generate business value
