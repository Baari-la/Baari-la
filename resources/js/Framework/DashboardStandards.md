# DIGESTEX DASHBOARD STANDARDS

## Version 1.0

---

# Purpose

All Digestex dashboards must provide executive-level intelligence rather than raw data.

A dashboard should help users understand:

What happened?

Why did it happen?

What should I do next?

---

# Dashboard Philosophy

Every dashboard follows the same structure.

```text
Header

↓

Summary Cards

↓

Charts

↓

AI Executive Brief

↓

Related Intelligence

↓

Business Actions
```

# Dashboard Goal

A Digestex dashboard is successful when users can make business decisions within minutes, not after manually analyzing raw data.

Dashboards transform industrial data into executive intelligence.
