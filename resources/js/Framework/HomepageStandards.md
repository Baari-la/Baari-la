# DIGESTEX HOMEPAGE STANDARDS

## Version 1.0

---

# Purpose

The Homepage is not a marketing website.

The Homepage is the Executive Gateway
to the Global Textile Industry Ecosystem.

Within the first 30 seconds,
visitors should understand

Who Digestex is

What Digestex provides

Why Digestex is different

What business value they receive.

---

# Homepage Philosophy

The homepage should answer

What is happening in the industry?

↓

What opportunities exist?

↓

Who can help?

↓

What action should be taken?
